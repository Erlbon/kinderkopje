Compare teams' UCI World Ranking points across seasons, and month by month against last year. Teams are matched by UCI's own team id, so renamed teams stay one line.

1. Drag this link to your bookmarks bar: Capture UCI table 2. Open a UCI DataRide team ranking page, pick the season and date with the page's own dropdowns, and press the bookmark on every page of the table (40 teams per page). It copies only what is on screen and makes no requests. 3. Press Download collection in its box, then open that file here.

Everything is kept in the shared database (see the Data page). __VERSION__
