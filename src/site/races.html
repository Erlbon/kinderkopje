Capture the point-scoring results of each race from UCI, one race at a time across the years. Every capture keeps only the rows that score points.

1. Drag this link to your bookmarks bar: Capture UCI race 2. Add each year's UCI hub page (for example "2025 UCI WorldTour", saved from your browser) to the queue. UCI gives every edition of a race its own number, so each year needs its own hub page; the queue lines the editions up by race name. 3. Open an edition from the queue. On the race page open Final Classification (and any stage you want), then press the bookmark. It copies only what is on screen and makes no requests. 4. Press Download collection in its box, and open the file here.

Everything is kept in the shared database (see the Data page). __VERSION__
