Help collect UCI race results

What to do

  1. Your name (so your work is credited, optional):
  2. For each race in the list below: click open on UCI. On the race page click Final Classification so the results table opens (for a stage race also open the stages you were asked for).
  3. Save the page: press Ctrl+S (Mac: Cmd+S) and choose Webpage, Complete (Firefox: Web Page, complete). The file name does not matter. It must be saved after the results are open.
  4. Drop all the saved files here (or pick them). The list below ticks off each race it could read.
  5. Download your results and send that one small file to the person who sent you this link.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; the results stay in this browser until you press Forget everything. Only the rows that score UCI points are kept.
